#The craftsman

Oleh is a senior product designer with nearly a decade of shaping interfaces that people actually enjoy using. Guided by empathy and sharpened by data, he has a rare talent: taking something tangled and making it feel obvious.

At Doorstead he led product design for property management — the kind of unglamorous, high-stakes domain where great design quietly changes everything. Conversion rates more than doubled under his watch. That's not luck; that's craft.
